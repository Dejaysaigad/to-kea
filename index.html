✦ A MESSAGE JUST FOR YOU ✦

To a Strong and Inspiring Woman 💜

Alam ko na hindi madali ang buhay na pinagdadaanan mo. As the breadwinner of your family, you carry so many responsibilities.

Your father is sick and can no longer work, your mother takes care of him, one of your siblings has their own family, and your other sibling is a PWD who cannot hear or speak.

You even had to stop studying for a while just to help your family. Hindi lahat kayang gawin ang mga sakripisyong ginagawa mo, but you continue to work hard despite all the struggles.

I just want you to know that you are a strong and amazing woman. Kahit mahirap ang buhay, patuloy kang lumalaban para sa mga taong mahal mo.

Always remember that your sacrifices are meaningful, and your dreams still matter, too.

Don't forget to take care of yourself. You deserve to rest, to be happy, and to achieve your own dreams someday.

Keep going, because this difficult chapter is not the end of your story.

Someday, all your hard work and sacrifices will be part of the beautiful story you will tell.

Never lose hope. God sees your efforts, your struggles, and your love for your family. Keep believing that better days are coming.

You are stronger than you think! 💜

Keep going!

Fighting!!!!!!!!
